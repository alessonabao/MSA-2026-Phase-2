# AI Prompt Engineering Log: En Garde Auckland

This log captures the evolution of prompts used to generate the "En Garde Auckland" fencing club hub.

## 1. Initial Concept & Layout
**Prompt:**
> "Provide a web application design for a fencing club in Auckland. Focus on a high-fidelity hub for discovery, member onboarding, and resource management. Use a minimalist, high-contrast aesthetic with brand blue (#214576) and charcoal (#1a1c1e)."

## 2. Event Discovery Iterations
**Prompt (Filtering):**
> "I want the events to be like a scrollable feed, while having the filters seen at the right side. Include: Keyword Search (Placeholder: 'Keywords...'), Weapon Type, Event Type, Skill Level, and a large interactive Calendar picker."

**Prompt (Compact Cards):**
> "Can you make the event cards more compact and change the grid to 4 columns instead of 3? In the events card, include: title, date & time, venue, description, weapon type, and skill level."

## 3. Beginner Resources & Technical Detail
**Prompt (Weapon Masterclass):**
> "Redesign the Resources page into a Beginner's Portal. Add specific technical specifications to weapon diagrams (weight, length, guard, target area). Add a glossary of fencing terms (Riposte, Flèche, Piste) with illustrative icons."

**Prompt (Video Integration):**
> "Redesign the Beginner Fencer's Guide to transition from static imagery to an interactive video-first experience. Specifically integrate demos about the 'En Garde' position and footwork."

## 4. Member Profile & Shadcn Styling
**Prompt (Participation Focus):**
> "Create a profile page showing bio, weapon of choice, skill level, and badges gained from attending events. Keep the design simple and rounded in line with Shadcn UI components. Use tabs in the style of Shadcn UI."

## 5. System-Level Refinements
**Prompt (Theme Switching):**
> "Add a light and dark mode toggle icon next to the notification bell. Ensure the dark mode uses #1a1c1e as the background and maintain high-contrast legibility for all text components."
